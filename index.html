05 — REFLECTION

What Usrah Taught Me

Usrah taught me that learning about Islam should not stop at knowing the basics. There is always more to understand, question and reflect upon.

Learning about Rukun Iman and Rukun Islam helped me understand that faith is connected to our actions. Knowledge should eventually lead to better amal.

The experience reminded me that becoming a better person is a continuous process. We learn, reflect, improve and continue the journey.
